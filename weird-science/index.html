Nine everyday mysteries. Three panels each. No lectures.

01

Phillips and flathead ruled forever. Then every new screw became a star. Why?

THE WEIRD

Two heads. Five centuries. Nobody asked why.

THE WHY

Impact drivers hit like hammers. Phillips bits jump ship.

THE VERDICT
Partly True

The star didn't beat Phillips. Torque did.

SOURCES ▸
  • Cam out, 1933 patent: "no tendency to cam out"
  • iFixit: Bit History, the Phillips
  • Canadian Encyclopedia: Robertson 1908
  • Torx, 1967
02

Why do shoelaces untie themselves?

THE WEIRD

Double knot. Still undone by lunch.

THE WHY

Stomp loosens. Swing whips. Two strides, gone.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

Square bow holds. Granny bow quits.

SOURCES ▸
  • UC Berkeley, 2017 study
  • NPR coverage
03

Microwaves can't get out the door. So how does light get in?

THE WEIRD

You can watch the soup. The waves can't watch you.

THE WHY

Long wave bounces. Tiny wave slips through.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

A metal cage, working in reverse.

SOURCES ▸
  • The Straight Dope
  • SlashGear: the mesh door
04

What is the tiny hole in airplane windows for?

THE WEIRD

A hole. In the window. Of a plane.

THE WHY

Outer pane takes the push. Middle pane waits as spare.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

Pressure balanced. Fog vented. Spare pane ready.

SOURCES ▸
  • Gizmodo, GKN Aerospace engineer
  • TIME, pilot Mark Vanhoenacker
05

Why does bread go stale faster in the fridge?

THE WEIRD

Fridge bread. Sad bread.

THE WHY

Starch recrystallises. Fridge temp is its favourite.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

Counter today. Freezer tomorrow. Never the fridge.

SOURCES ▸
  • ScienceABC on retrogradation
  • Chowhound, 2021 fridge study
06

How does the gas pump know when your tank is full?

THE WEIRD

It just knows. How does it know?

THE WHY

Fuel covers a tiny hole. Vacuum snaps the valve shut.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

No chips. No wires. Just plumbing.

SOURCES ▸
  • ScienceABC
  • US Patent 4,098,306
07

Was QWERTY designed to slow typists down?

THE WEIRD

Legend says: scattered keys, so the bars wouldn't jam.

THE WHY

Telegraph operators shaped the keys. For speed.

THE VERDICT
Myth Busted

Not slow. Just stuck with it.

SOURCES ▸
  • Smithsonian: the QWERTY legend
  • Language Log on the Kyoto study
08

Water is clear. So why do wet things look darker?

THE WEIRD

Same concrete. Darker when it rains.

THE WHY

Light gets trapped under the film. Each bounce, more absorbed.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

Less light escapes. Your eye calls it dark.

SOURCES ▸
  • NC State, total internal reflection
  • Lekner & Dorf, 1988
09

Why does ice float? And why should you care?

THE WEIRD

A solid floating on its own liquid. That's rare.

THE WHY

Freezing builds an open lattice. More room, less dense.

THE VERDICT
Resolved

Lakes freeze from the top. Fish live. So do we.

SOURCES ▸
  • Why ice floats, with the numbers
  • Climate TRACE
